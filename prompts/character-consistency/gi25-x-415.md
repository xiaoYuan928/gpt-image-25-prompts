```text

COMPOSITION
Create a portrait image with a 3:4 aspect ratio, divided into two equally sized horizontal panels stacked vertically.
Use a first-person perspective from someone seated at a desk. An open laptop sits in the center, with its keyboard and the desktop clearly visible.
The character’s head, shoulders, and one arm emerge through the laptop screen, while the rest of their body remains inside the screen, creating a convincing “breaking the fourth wall” illusion.

Make the character’s face the main focal point, with the joined hands creating natural foreground depth and foreshortening.

TWO-PANEL EXPRESSION CHANGE
Top panel: The character looks toward the viewer with a slightly surprised or curious expression. Add a small hand-drawn exclamation mark beside their head.
Bottom panel: Keep the same camera angle and hand-holding pose, but change the expression to a happy smile that suits the character’s personality. Add a small hand-drawn heart.
Keep the character design, background, object placement, and lighting consistent across both panels. The main change should be the facial expression.

CHARACTER-MATCHED ENVIRONMENT
Design the room, furniture, desktop, and decorations to suit the character’s fictional world, identity, outfit, and signature colors.
Give the setting a clear connection to the character. Do not reuse a generic room or a fixed set of props for every character. Do not add ceramic jars, vases, or tea sets by default.

Keep other props minimal, relevant, and secondary to the character.
Retain the laptop as the visual bridge between the real world and the character’s world.

VISUAL STYLE
Preserve the character’s illustrated style, while rendering the room, laptop, desk, and viewer’s hand with realistic photographic textures.
Unify the two styles through consistent lighting, contact shadows, and perspective.
Use refined detail, soft lighting, subtle background blur, and a warm atmosphere with natural, gentle interaction.

QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

No extra limbs, unintended duplicate characters, misplaced accessories, text, captions, brand logos, or watermarks.
```
