### Travel-Memory Emoji Scrapbook from Uploaded Photo

```text
Transform the uploaded photo into a premium **travel-memory Emoji scrapbook poster**.

Use the uploaded photo as the primary visual reference. Preserve the important subjects, people, objects, environment, composition, colors, atmosphere, and recognizable details accurately.

Do not alter the identity, facial features, body proportions, clothing, or important structure of the main subject.

Create a **vertical 3:4 editorial scrapbook composition**, divided horizontally into two clean, balanced sections.

## TOP 50% — ORIGINAL MEMORY

Display the original photograph prominently.

Keep it realistic and photographic, with only subtle enhancement:

- warm natural color grading
- soft cinematic light
- slightly elevated contrast
- realistic skin and materials
- gentle film-like texture
- refined travel-editorial photography aesthetic

Do not over-retouch the image.

Do not turn the photograph into an illustration.

The upper half should still feel like an authentic candid memory captured on camera.

---

## BOTTOM 50% — EMOJI MEMORY BOARD

Transform the most recognizable elements from the photograph into a curated collection of **6–9 custom Emoji-style icons**.

Automatically identify the strongest visual memories in the uploaded photo, such as:

- food or drinks
- buildings or landmarks
- clothing or accessories
- flowers, trees or plants
- clouds, sun, moon or weather
- vehicles
- animals
- street objects
- travel items
- meaningful objects held by the subject
- distinctive colors or visual motifs

Every Emoji must clearly originate from something visible in the uploaded photograph.

Do not introduce unrelated objects.

### EMOJI STYLE

Render each extracted element as a polished, dimensional **premium Emoji sticker**:

- clean rounded forms
- expressive simplified shapes
- soft dimensional volume
- subtle glossy highlights
- gentle ambient shadows
- rich but tasteful colors
- slightly playful proportions
- crisp edges
- highly recognizable silhouettes
- modern mobile-Emoji aesthetic
- sophisticated rather than childish
- visually consistent across all icons

The Emoji should feel like a beautifully designed custom icon set inspired by the photograph.

Avoid mixing different Emoji styles.

Do not use hand-drawn sketches, watercolor, pencil, ink, line art, photorealism, or realistic miniature objects.

---

## LAYOUT

Place the Emoji icons on a warm ivory / soft off-white editorial paper background.

Arrange them in a refined loose **3 × 3 memory grid**.

The grid does not need to be perfectly rigid.

Allow slight variation in:

- Emoji scale
- vertical position
- rotation
- spacing

to create a natural scrapbook feeling.

Keep generous negative space.

Do not place visible boxes around individual Emoji.

Use extremely subtle shadows beneath selected Emoji so they feel like small digital stickers resting on the page.

Add only a few tiny supporting Emoji accents where appropriate, such as:

✨ ⭐️ ☁️ 💛 🌿 🫧 ☀️ 💫

Use them sparingly.

The composition should remain elegant and uncluttered.

---

## TITLE

At the top of the Emoji section, add one short editorial title inspired by the photograph.

Examples:

“PARIS MEMORIES”
“SUMMER DAYS”
“WEEKEND MOMENTS”
“LITTLE THINGS”
“SWEET MEMORIES”
“ONE PERFECT DAY”

Use clean, minimal typography.

The title may combine:

a refined serif or sans-serif typeface  
+ one small Emoji accent.

For example:

SUMMER DAYS ☀️

or

PARIS MEMORIES ✨

Do not use elaborate handwritten lettering.

---

## OPTIONAL MICRO LABELS

Under only 3–5 of the main Emoji icons, add extremely short labels of no more than 1–2 words.

Examples:

morning coffee  
blue skies  
little flowers  
sweet treat  
city lights  
slow morning  
sunset walk

Keep the text small, subtle, editorial, and secondary to the Emoji.

If accurate typography cannot be generated cleanly, omit the labels rather than creating incorrect text.

---

## BOTTOM CAPTION

At the very bottom, add one tiny sentimental line.

Examples:

“a day worth keeping ♡”

“little moments, big memories”

“saved in my camera roll ♡”

“wish I could replay this day”

“one of those days ✨”

Keep it understated and elegant.

premium travel editorial
```
